Historical Documents

Explore the history of the Nestlé-LeMur Company, the American hair care and beauty company formed in 1928 through the merger of C. Nestle & Co. and the Le Mur Company.
 

This digital archive brings together original historical documents, including newspaper articles, professional hairdressing journals, advertisements, patents, company publications and other contemporary sources.
 

The collection documents the development of permanent waving, the company's hair care products, technical innovations and international expansion. It also provides historical material relating to Charles (Karl Ludwig) Nessler, the pioneer of the permanent wave.
 

The archive is continuously expanded as new historical sources become available.

Published in New York in 1935 by the Nestlé-LeMur Company, The Basic Science of Hair Treatments was prepared for use in the company's instruction centers.
 

The publication provides a historical insight into professional hairdressing education and the scientific understanding of hair treatments during the 1930s.
 

Featuring a foreword by Florence E. Wall, F.A.I.C., this original publication is an important source for exploring the history of hair care, professional training and the development of the Nestlé-LeMur Company.
